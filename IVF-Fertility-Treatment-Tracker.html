Guide & Safety

What this app does — and what it deliberately does not do.

How to use it

1. Create a cycle

Add the treatment type, clinic, start date and key expected dates.

2. Copy clinic instructions

Enter medication and appointment information exactly as provided by your fertility team.

3. Add monitoring results

Save follicle measurements, lining and bloodwork after each clinic update.

4. Follow retrieval and embryos

Use the lab summary for totals and the embryo tracker for individual IDs, grades and status.

5. Back up regularly

Data is stored locally on the device/browser, so export a backup after meaningful updates.

When symptoms need medical attention

This section is informational, not diagnostic.

Contact your fertility clinic promptly if you are unwell or have concerning symptoms during treatment. Urgent warning symptoms described by major fertility/health authorities include significant abdominal pain or swelling, persistent vomiting, faintness, reduced urination, difficulty breathing, chest/upper-back pain, or other rapidly worsening symptoms.

For emergency symptoms, use your local emergency service. This app cannot assess symptom severity or replace your clinic's instructions.

Privacy

The standalone app stores data in the browser's local storage. No account or cloud sync is required. Anyone with access to the same browser profile/device may be able to access the saved data, so use an appropriately secured device.
